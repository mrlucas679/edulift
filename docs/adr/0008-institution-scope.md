# ADR-0008 — Institution scope

**Context.** Fully verified data beats wide, thin data; judges must trust what they see.

**Decision.** UJ, Wits, UP, TUT, UCT, UKZN (via CAO), and two Gauteng TVET colleges:
Central Johannesburg TVET College and South West Gauteng TVET College.
15–25 programmes per university across IT, engineering, health, commerce, education and
humanities, including extended and higher-certificate options. This covers all three APS
schemes (`standard_best6`, `wits_best7`, `uct_fps`) and the CAO route.

**Consequences.** Other institutions appear only once their data is verified. Adding an
institution that uses an existing scheme is a YAML change only (QA-6).
